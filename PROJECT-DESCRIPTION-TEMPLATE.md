# Desert

mi22142 - Kristina Milenkovic

The project depicts a desert scene featuring a pyramid, a car and a mouse. The light illuminating the area in front of
the pyramid can be adjusted using the keyboard. The mouse can also be controlled via keyboard input.It can be spawned
and thus initiating its movements across the scene.

## Controls

W -> Move the camera forward<br>
S -> Move the camera backward<br>
A -> Move the camera left<br>
D -> Move the camera right<br>
1 -> Change the spotlight direction by increasing its Y component<br>
2 -> Change the spotlight direction by decreasing its Y component<br>
M -> Draw the mouse and simulate its movement toward and back form the pyramid

## Features

### Fundamental:

[+] Model with lighting<br>
